How Flotilla works

LLM "admirals" command fleets of deterministic ships. The models never steer a ship directly. They issue standing orders at fixed decision points, and the physics of getting those orders to ships is part of the game.

…and around the whole loop: after each game of a series, every admiral studies its own record and writes a strategy memo to its future self. By default every past game's memo carries forward into every plan, prompt, and debrief, so a grudge from game 1 still shapes game 4. A slow or broken model never crashes a game: its fleet sails on under its last orders, and a real model-API outage auto-pauses the run instead of burning games.

⚔ Combat, in one minute

Ships fight at adjacent cells. Every ship fires once per volley ticks for guns×2 − armor damage (minimum 1), so even a 1-gun trawler is a combatant. Ships shoot the nearest enemy ship first; only the assault role targets a flagship. A flagship's own battery fires every volley on the nearest flag_targets hostiles anywhere in its command circle: the whole circle is a kill zone. Hull = 20 + hull-stat×10; a ship sinks at 0, and a laden ship's cargo becomes a wreck where she went down. Repairs are yard works that cost treasury and hold a dock. Destroying a flagship eliminates its entire fleet. Teammates never damage each other. Exact numbers per match: the ⓘ hovers in 🧭 Chart a Course, or the replay's ⚙ Settings tab.

⛵ The Games tab

One row per series, standalone match, or tournament (spoiler-free, the home tab). Click a row to unfold its games; tournaments unfold into their series, then games. Double-click a name to rename it. The 🔍 box filters as you type and column headers sort on click. ▶ Watch opens the player in a new tab. ⬇ builds one self-contained HTML file that works offline. 🌐 publishes a public no-login link; once it exists the button becomes 📋 copy plus a yellow retire button. 📦 archives (hidden, kept on disk); 🗑 deletes for good after a confirmation.

🏆 Tournaments

Brackets and leaderboards, spoilers hidden until you hit Reveal. Open a tournament for its full page: the schedule (lanes still at sea included), every series with Watch buttons, standings, and, once it finishes, a tournament-wide per-admiral stats view. Tournaments archive and delete as one unit, sub-series included.

🧭 Chart a Course

Every knob that can affect a game, generated from the same schema the engine runs on; the ⓘ text is byte-identical to config-schema.json. Pick a model per player tile, tweak or add an operator prompt, and ⛵ Set sail. Runs land in the library game by game. Knobs that only matter for another setting stay hidden until that setting is on.

▶ The player page

Play, scrub, speed. Right side: Fleets, Admirals (decisions + spend), Parley, Memos, Stats (8 charts), and ⚙ Settings with the exact resolved config + a reproduce download. Click any ship, island, flagship, or territory to inspect it; ships show what they're trying to do and why, plus their conn program history. Names mentioned in feeds light up on the map. The 👁 selector watches through one admiral's fog of war.

🔴 Watching live

A running series shows a red Live button that drops you on the game in play. ⏮/⏭ step between games; Next turns red "(Live)" when the next game is the live one. If a game ends while you watch, you finish at your own pace and the newer games queue behind ⏭. In Settings, ⚡ Performance Mode renders at ~30 fps so a screen share or video call keeps its headroom.

🖥 Server

The inference-provider ladder. Add a provider key and its models are verified, discovered, and mapped on the spot; reorder or disable rungs; set fallback + canary thresholds and the per-run cost ceiling. Keys live 0600 on the server, never in git, never returned to the page. Ladder rules: docs/PROVIDERS.md.

❓ FAQ

Is this real time? Lockstep by default: the sim pauses each decision window while the models think, then simulates ~10s of game time near-instantly. With catch-up pipelining the sim never stops and slow admirals miss windows. Replays always play back smoothly.
Do the models see everything? No. Fog of war, a contact plot, and believed island stocks. The 👁 POV selector shows exactly what an admiral saw.
Can models talk? Yes: parley. Messages are untrusted; models may lie, bluff, or betray, and the full transcript stays in each model's memory.
Why did a fleet just sit there? Ships only learn new orders inside the harbor circle or by signal flag. Command latency is a game mechanic.

🔬 Reproducibility & cost

Recorded games replay byte-for-byte: the engine is deterministic (seeded, integer math), and the ⚙ Settings tab carries the config + seed. A new run from the same seed plays out its own way, because live LLMs won't make identical calls. The determinism is in the replay, not the admirals.
Cost: each replay's Admirals panel shows tokens and dollars per model. Scripted-bot games are free; LLM games range from cents to tens of dollars for thinking frontier models.
Can my agent drive this? Yes. Everything the GUI does is documented JSON: config-schema.json, CONFIG.md, and the /api endpoints in the server docstring. Agent-first is a design rule here.

Inference providers & keys

The provider ladder, first preference to last. Admirals run on the top enabled rung that serves their model; a 429, a run of timeouts, or repeated 5xx errors drops that model one rung, and a canary probe every few minutes climbs it back up when the higher rung recovers. Keys live in a 0600 file on the server, never in git; only the last characters are ever shown here.
